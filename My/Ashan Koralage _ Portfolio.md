## About Me

### Web Developer & Designer

Hi, I’m Ashan Charuka, a second-year design student at the University of Colombo School of Computing with a love for turning curious ideas into something people can see, feel, and remember. I’m still learning, and I like it that way: every project is a chance to experiment, make mistakes, and come out with something better than I imagined.
