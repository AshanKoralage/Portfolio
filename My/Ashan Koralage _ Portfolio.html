About Me

Web Developer & Designer

Hi, I’m Ashan Charuka, a second-year design student at the University of Colombo School of Computing with a love for turning curious ideas into something people can see, feel, and remember. I’m still learning, and I like it that way: every project is a chance to experiment, make mistakes, and come out with something better than I imagined. My work blends illustration, photography, web design, and writing with a fresh, playful perspective, and I’m always looking for new ways to tell a story visually. Outside of class, you’ll find me sketching in cafés, exploring new music, and filming short clips. I’m currently looking for projects where I can grow, contribute, and create something meaningful. Take a look around, and let’s make something great together.
